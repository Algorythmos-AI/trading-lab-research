# LL-0002 — Stops inside noise lose to costs

S3 (1-min VWAP pullback, stop a cent under VWAP) lost −0.12R/trade over ~45k large-cap trades: 70% stopped
out, median favourable excursion 0.8R. A setup's stop must sit beyond the instrument's typical noise
(e.g. ≥1 ATR of the entry timeframe) or the edge is consumed by whipsaw + slippage.
