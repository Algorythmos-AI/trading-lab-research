5. GAP AND GO STRATEGY: ENTRY & STOP RULES
================================================================================
- Entry Trigger Variations:
  1. Pre-Market High / Flat Top Breakout: Buy as/seconds before price breaks pre-market high or flat-top apex. Requires support holding above moving averages, alignment near whole/half dollars, and Level 2 ask absorption.
  2. 1-Minute Opening Range Breakout (ORB): Enter when the 2nd 1-minute candle breaks above the high of the 1st 1-minute candle.
  3. First Pullback (Bull Flag / 9 EMA): Enter on the first 1-minute or 5-minute candle making a new high coming out of a bull flag or 9 EMA pullback.
  4. Red-to-Green Move: Buy the first candle making a new high as price re-claims the market opening price from below.
- Stop-Loss Placement:
  - Pre-Market High / Bull Flag: Stop at the low of the pullback/pattern.
  - 1-Minute ORB: Stop at the low of the first 1-minute candle.
  - Red-to-Green: Mental or hard stop at Low of Day (LOD).
  - Typical Stop Distance: $0.10 to $0.20 per share.
- Profit Targets & Exits:
  - First Target: Sell 50% at 2:1 R/R or key resistance (whole/half dollars). Adjust stop to breakeven.
  - Final Exit: Exit remaining position if a 5-minute candle breaks a previous low, price breaks below 9 EMA, or Level 2 buying momentum dries up.

6. MICRO PULLBACK STRATEGY: ENTRY & STOP RULES
================================================================================
- Core Concept: Aggressive entry technique for hyper-strong momentum stocks where 1-min or 5-min pullbacks are too slow.
- Entry Triggers:
  - Appears as a single small red candle or bottom wick on 1-min / 5-min charts.
  - Sub-1-minute execution (10-second chart): Enter as price breaks out of micro-consolidation/flat top on 10-second chart with volume surge.
  - Aligns near key psychological levels (half and whole dollars).
- Stop-Loss Placement:
  - Strict stop at the low of the micro pullback candle/wick.
  - Immediate exit if setup fails to resolve instantly.
- Profit Target:
  - Quick $0.50 move per share toward the next half or whole dollar mark.
