### In this hotfix

| Area | Pull requests | What it is |
|---|---|---|
| Manage card | #452 | EXIT when the bid is at or under the −25% premium stop on two fresh OPRA quotes in a row, latched for the session; the day pill and banner count open losses at the bid (display only); "I'm in" takes an optional strike, so the stop is watched from the fill |
| Manage card, review fixes | this hotfix | A fresh quote too one-sided or too wide to value still reads against the stop: when even the ask is at or under it, it counts as under (a collapsing option fires the EXIT); a real bid above it counts as above; a quote that cannot say (the stop inside the spread, an empty or crossed quote) never fires and turns HOLD, TRAIL or SELL HALF into CHECK, which keeps reading its quote and is cleared only by a quote that reads the stop. The count and the latch belong to the trade's stop level (with a few cents' slack, so the card changing hands keeps the EXIT); one upstream quote counts once (keyed on its own time and prices), and again after 10 s if it has not moved; a fire stands for a fill up to 2 minutes after it (the note-to-journal window); the count is kept per day; a card whose option chain is off, failing or still loading after 15 s says its −25% stop is not watched |

The hotfix touches no file the evaluator or the signal hashes. `gen-engine.mjs --check` and `gen-signal.mjs --check`
both pass against `main`, and `SIGNAL_CODE_HASH` is unchanged.

## How to release

- After a hotfix merges into `main`, open a pull request from `main` into `integration` and merge it with a merge
  commit, so the next release carries it.
